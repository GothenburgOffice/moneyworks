# Code of conduct

MoneyWorks is a small, owner-maintained project. This applies to every space where it is
discussed: issues, pull requests, comments and any other channel the project uses.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Disagree with the work, not the person.
- Keep to the topic of the issue or pull request, and keep it constructive.
- Never post other people's personal or financial information, and never post your own:
  no account numbers, tokens, passwords or real financial figures in issues or logs.
- Accept that the maintainer decides what is built and merged, and that review time is
  limited (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

## What is not accepted

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks of any kind
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention
- Publishing private information about someone else
- Spam, advertising or repeated off-topic comments
- Anything else that a reasonable person would think inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, close or lock issues and pull requests, and
block people from the repository, with or without warning, when behavior breaks these rules.

To report something, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request
itself (it reaches the repository admins privately). Vulnerabilities go through the private route
in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not here.
