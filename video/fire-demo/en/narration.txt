Let's build a simple retirement plan in Money Working. We'll follow one example, from your starting point to the savings you need.

First, set your timeline. Enter your current age, your preferred retirement age, and how far ahead you want to plan.

Next, add your monthly spending, the portfolio you already have, and what you save each month. Use the same currency for every amount.

Now include a pension. Set the monthly payout and the ages it covers. Save closes the editor and adds the payout to a compact list. You can use the pen to change it later.

Choose Calculate. The retirement view shows the portfolio needed at your chosen retirement age. The chart then follows that capital as you spend and receive pension income.

Switch to Savings plan. Here, compare your projected savings with the retirement target. If there's a gap, the calculator shows the monthly saving needed to close it.

Change an assumption and calculate again to explore a different plan. Your inputs stay on this page. Saving your plan is optional.
